### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Removed unused `pendingCareer*` destructure from useCareerManager call**
- **Found during:** Task 1/2 (`npx tsc -b --noEmit` after wiring `useCareerManager`)
- **Issue:** Plan's `<interfaces>` block shows `pendingCareerLeagueFixture, pendingCareerBracketStage, pendingCareerBracketIdx` destructured from `useCareerManager`'s return, but no task in this plan actually reads them anywhere in `App.tsx` — they're only consumed inside the hook's own handlers (`handleCareerLeagueMatchResult`, `handleCareerBracketMatchResult`). - **Fix:** Dropped those three bindings from the `useCareerManager({...})` destructure in `App.tsx`; the hook still returns them for any future consumer, just not one App.tsx needs.
